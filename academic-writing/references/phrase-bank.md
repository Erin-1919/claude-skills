# Academic Writing Phrase Bank

Extracted from the author's published papers (2019-2026). Use these as templates, adapting the specific content while preserving the sentence architecture.

## Introduction Openers

### Broad Context Starters
- "With advances in [field/technology], [researchers/scientists] have become more interested in [topic]."
- "[Topic] has been recognized as [description], addressing [problem]."
- "During the long period of [field] development, [phenomena] are commonly [verb]ed by [approach]."
- "[Systems/Methods] emerged as consistent structures to fulfill the needs of [purpose]."
- "Recent studies have shown the importance of [topic] in revolutionizing [domain]."
- "[Greenhouse gases / Natural hazards / etc.] are critical drivers of [impact], [elaboration]."

### Narrowing to Problem
- "Although [X] has demonstrated both [A] and [B] development, [it has] not been applied widely to [purpose]."
- "Before the wide application of [X] in solving real-world problems, the potential [limitations] should be investigated and documented."
- "However, two persistent limitations affect their interpretability and reuse."
- "Nevertheless, there remains a demand for [solution] to effectively [verb] [object]."
- "Despite [progress], there remains a gap in [specific area]."

### Stating Objectives
- "The objectives of this study were 1) to determine [X], and 2) to test if [Y]."
- "The specific objectives of this research are (1) to demonstrate [X], and (2) to identify [Y]."
- "This study aimed to explore the application of [X] in [domain]."
- "This paper aims to address this need by [approach]."
- "This study demonstrates the application of [X] to construct [Y], replacing [Z]."
- "In this paper, we aimed to conduct [analysis] in [framework]."

### Paper Roadmap
- "The remainder of this paper is organized as follows. Section 2 [describes/reviews]... Section 3 [introduces]... Section 4 [demonstrates]... Section 5 [presents]... Section 6 [discusses]... Section 7 [concludes]..."
- "The remainder of this paper is arranged as follows. Section 2 shows... In Section 3, we explain..."

## Methods Phrases

### Study Area Description
- "The study area was located in [region], [geographic context]."
- "The study area is about [size], located in [location], and covers [features]."
- "This paper demonstrates the [process] on [framework] and tests the process over a [size] area around [location]."

### Data Sources
- "Datasets for all the other influencing variables were available through various sources, and necessary preprocessing was done before analyses, as described in Table 1."
- "A total of [N] explanatory factors including [categories] were investigated."
- "[Data] were obtained from [source] at [resolution] with [reference system]."
- "Raw geospatial data were obtained in raster or vector formats."

### Analytical Methods
- "The analysis was carried out using [software/library] version [X]."
- "We followed the [method] proposed by [Author] (Year) who managed to [achievement]."
- "This study adopted the [algorithm] proposed by [Author] et al. (Year) and adjusted it in the [context]."
- "[Method] was used to determine the most influential predictors in forecasting [outcome]."
- "Experiments were carried out across multiple [scales/resolutions] with various [conditions]."
- "Experiments at multiple resolutions were carried out over [N] study areas with different levels of [property]."

### Equations and Variables
- "where [variable] is the [definition], in which [elaboration]."
- "The specific [parameter] in the [context] is adjusted from the calculation in [original context], and is calculated as (Eq. N):"

## Results Phrases

### Presenting Findings
- "Results showed that [finding]."
- "The [metric] was [value], which was [N]% higher/lower than [comparison]."
- "The results show that at [condition], the [metric] remains [description], with [values]."
- "A cell-based pair-wise comparison showed a strong positive correlation between [A] and [B]."
- "The grid resolutions as well as the [property] had a clear effect on the computed [output]."

### Metric Comparisons
- "The [metric] was [value], which was [comparison] than [baseline]."
- "On the [benchmark], [system] achieves [metric] of [value]."
- "As shown in Table [N], [finding]."

### Quantitative Descriptions
- "The total [measure] peaked in [year] at [value] and gradually declined to [value] in [year]."
- "This decline was primarily driven by reductions in [category]."
- "Collectively, these [items] accounted for [N]% of [total]."
- "The [parameter] ranged from [min] to [max], with a mean of [value]."

## Discussion Phrases

### Interpreting Results
- "For [purpose], [variable] was the most important variable."
- "These patterns align with [phenomenon] observed in [other context]."
- "These findings indicate that [implication]."
- "The cross-framework comparison shows broad agreement in [areas] at coarse scales."
- "Sensitivity analyses showed that [finding]."

### Comparing to Literature
- "This is consistent with [Author] et al. (Year) who found..."
- "This aligns with prior work showing that [finding] (Author et al., Year)."
- "Previous studies also found [similar/contrasting] relationships between [X] and [Y]."
- "However, the findings about [topic] in this study do not necessarily apply to other [contexts]."
- "Some previous studies also found [relationship] (Author and Author, Year; Author et al., Year)."

### Limitations and Caveats
- "Several methodological limitations should be noted."
- "However, our [analysis/approach] [limitation]. Future work should..."
- "The lack of stronger overall effects can be attributed to [reason]."
- "The quantified relationships found, however, were not consistent across [condition]."
- "This should be more of a concern in the case of [scenario] due to [reason]."

### Practical Implications
- "The [framework] provides immediate practical value for [application]."
- "Understanding which variables influence [phenomenon] assists in [practical use]."
- "The study outcomes can serve as useful guidelines in the selection among [options]."
- "This research can serve as a guide for future development of [application]."

## Conclusion Phrases

### Opening Summary
- "This study [verb]ed [what was done] and [key achievement]."
- "Using [method], we determined the variables that influenced [phenomenon]."
- "This paper used [framework] as an example to show the process of [achievement]."

### Closing Statements
- "Future investigation into [X] is recommended."
- "This research can serve as a guide for future development of [application], providing [benefits]."
- "Several future research directions regarding [topics] are presented."
- "The developed operations can be used in [application] in the real world."
- "Beyond the case study, we position [framework] as [broader contribution]."

## Transition Patterns

### Between Paragraphs
- "In addition to [previous topic], [new topic]..."
- "Although [X] has been [state], [Y] has not been [state]."
- "Contrary to [X], [Y] provides [advantages]."
- "Other than [advantage], [subject] is also advantageous from [other aspects]."
- "The last few decades have witnessed the development of [technologies]."

### Within Paragraphs
- "Specifically, [detail]."
- "In particular, [emphasis]."
- "Nonetheless, [contrast]."
- "Accordingly, [consequence]."
- "In other words, [rephrasing]."
- "To name just a few, [examples]."
- "In our case, [specific application]."
